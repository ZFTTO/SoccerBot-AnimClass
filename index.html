齒輪箱怎麼傳動？How does the gearbox transfer motion?

逐件裝入，留意小齒輪和輸出軸朝哪邊。拖動畫面可轉換角度。Fit the parts one by one. Watch which way the small gears and output shaft face. Drag to orbit.

兩條履帶怎麼轉彎？How do two tracks steer the robot?

先猜車會向哪邊轉，再按左右方向鍵。同一個鍵再按一次，就會停下那一邊。Predict which way the robot will turn, then try the buttons. Press the same button again to stop that track.

蝸牛形凸輪怎麼帶動球叉？How does a snail cam move the fork?

拖動滑桿，看凸輪壓低球叉、壓縮內外兩根彈簧。凸輪轉過缺口時，彈簧回彈，把球叉向上推。Drag the slider to see the cam lower the fork and compress both springs. At the step, the springs rebound and push the fork up.
